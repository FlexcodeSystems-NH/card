Physical: the product is the hero PRODUCT-LED

  1. Brand + product photoThe source of truth
  2. Inspect + isolateCapture product details
  3. Message + art directionPlan scene and text zones
  4. GenerateCreate the advertising image
  5. Fidelity audit + reviewCompare with the source product

Why fidelity matters

For a clothing brand, a generated shirt that changes stitching or logo placement is unusable. Every image is compared with the real product before a person approves it.

SaaS: the message is the hero MESSAGE-LED

  1. Brand + ideaNo product photo required
  2. Message strategyCampaign angle and copy
  3. Creative directionScene, style and layout
  4. Generate + auditCheck text, facts, logo
  5. Real logo + reviewFinal human approval

Built for B2B

For software brands there is no product to photograph. The pipeline starts from the brand voice and a campaign idea, and audits every claim and logo before review.
