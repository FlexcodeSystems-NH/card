The problem

Students don't lack material. They lack a plan that fits their week.

Deadlines collide

Assignments and exams from different courses land in the same week.

Finding good resources takes time

Searching for the right material for each topic eats into study time.

Plans don't survive reality

Static timetables ignore how much time a student actually has.

The system

Specialised agents, one shared plan.

Each agent does one job well; an orchestrator combines their work into the weekly plan.
